Wander US LLC operates Moments in the Miles. This policy explains what we
collect, why, and what you can do about it. Plain language, no tricks.

**Analytics.** This site uses Google Analytics 4 to collect anonymous usage data — pages visited, time on page, general location (country/region), and device type. This data helps us understand what content is useful to readers. Google Analytics may use cookies to track return visits. No personally identifiable information is collected through analytics.

**Newsletter.** If you sign up for our newsletter, we collect your email address. We use Kit (formerly ConvertKit) to store that address and send updates, which means your email is held on their servers under their privacy policy. Kit's script runs on this site and may set a cookie. We do not sell or share your email with third parties. You can unsubscribe at any time via the link in any email we send, and unsubscribing removes you from the list.

**Affiliate links.** Some pages on this site carry affiliate links to travel booking partners. If you click one and go on to book, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. To make that work, the partner typically sets a cookie in your browser so a later booking can be credited back to us. That cookie belongs to them, is governed by their privacy policy, and we do not receive your booking details, payment information, or personal data from it — only aggregate reporting on whether a booking happened.

Every page carrying an affiliate link says so on the page itself. We only link to places we would send a friend, and a link never changes what we write.

**Contact form.** The form on our Work With Us page collects your name, email address, optional organisation, and your message. It is processed by Netlify Forms, which stores the submission and emails it to us. We use it to reply to you and for nothing else.

**Purchases.** The Leave Anyway Kit is sold through Gumroad. Gumroad handles the entire checkout, including your payment details — we never see or store your card information. What we receive is the record of the sale and the email address you purchased with, so we can deliver the product and support it. Gumroad's own privacy policy governs their handling of your data.

**Hosting.** This site is hosted by Netlify, which keeps standard server logs (including IP addresses) as part of delivering and protecting the site.

**Cookies, in one place.** The cookies set through this site come from Google Analytics, Kit, and our affiliate partners, as described above. We do not set advertising cookies of our own. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time; the site will still work, though analytics will not count your visit. If we ever add display advertising, we will update this policy before it goes live.

**No data selling.** We do not sell personal data. Full stop.

**Your choices.** You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time, block cookies in your browser, and use Google's [opt-out add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout) to exclude yourself from Analytics. If you want to know what we hold about you, or want it deleted, email us and we will sort it out.

**Children.** This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

**Changes.** We update this policy when what the site does changes. The date below tells you when we last did.

**Contact.** Questions about this policy? Email us at hey@momentsinthemiles.com.
